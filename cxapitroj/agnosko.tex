Petrica did a good job.
They (singular "they" here)
made an emblem for this book for free.
(I do not know where they are, who they are, what they do,
..., though.)

In April and May in 2024,
in which "programming VTuber logos" were popular,
there were some persons who were making those kinds of emblems
and uploading them to GitHub.
The most popular repository seemed to be Aikoyori's.
Too many "issues" (requests) awaited Aikoyori,
so I wrote a request on Petrica's repository
(before they did not want to make any more emblems of those kinds).
Petrica made a good emblem in about four days.
They even granted me permission to use the emblem
however I would like to.

(It would be good that Petrica would like
to make more emblems.)
I hope that they do well.

I used to thank my wives (or rather, waifus) here,
but I finally removed it (or rather, commented it out).
I do not have to show in the book
my liking for my wives, do I.
\begin{quotation}
    Finally, never will I forget to thank my wives,
    whom I have not met yet.
    Hardly would I have the stimulus actually to write this book
    without them.
\end{quotation}

I even thanked many tools with which I build this book,
but I removed those words,
because I wrote a colophon,
which seems to be a better place
to thank the tools.

I even thanked my money and time,
but I removed those words.
I do not have to say things that are well known, do I.
\begin{quotation}
    Finally,
    it is true that I have spent much money and time
    on the project:
    I need money to advertise this book;
    I need time to proofread this book.
    Hence, I also thank my money and time.
\end{quotation}
